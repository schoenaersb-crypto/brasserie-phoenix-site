A French-Belgian bistronomic brasserie facing the Mediterranean: meat grilled at your table on lava stone, seafood, homemade fresh pasta and a 5-course Tasting Menu. Glass-walled terrace with sea views and sunsets.

The Phoenix spirit

Like the bird that gives it its name, Brasserie Phoenix was born from fire. Grills on lava stone, meats flambéed over the spit: here the flame isn't decor, it is our language.

Facing the Mediterranean in Torrevieja, we blend the generosity of the South with the elegance of a true brasserie: seafood of the day, fresh homemade pasta, and exceptional meats chosen with care.

Every service is a rebirth: that of a place where time is taken, where fire brings people together, and where every plate tells of the sea and the embers.

Frequently asked questions

Where is Brasserie Phoenix?

Av. Desiderio Rodríguez 37, local 10, 03185 Torrevieja (Alicante, Spain), right on the seafront, with a glass-walled terrace facing the Mediterranean.

What are the opening hours?

Thursday to Tuesday, lunch and dinner; closed on Wednesdays. Kitchen 12:00–15:00 and 18:00–22:00; bar and dining room 11:00–midnight.

What kind of food do you serve?

French-Belgian bistronomic cooking: meat grilled on lava stone (Galician, Argentinian and Belgian beef, Duroc pork), seafood, fish, homemade fresh pasta and homemade desserts.

Do you have a tasting menu?

Yes: a 5-course Tasting Menu at €37.50 (seared scallops with Tripel Karmeliet reduction, pan-fried half foie gras, fresh salad, whole sea bass or half duck breast, pear in wine), with 3 or 5 glass wine pairings.

How do I book?

By phone or WhatsApp on +34 744 62 29 75, or through our website.

Is there a children's menu?

Yes, children's dishes at €12 (burger, nuggets, pasta).

What happens on Tuesday evenings?

A special night with DJ Kavari, with the full menu available.
